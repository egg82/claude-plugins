# Adoption vs. risk — the worked case

Open this when a recommendation is about to turn on risk findings and the people who must use the
tool haven't been asked about.

Worked case: a vet returned ten advisories on a cluster add-on, and the recommendation was
"don't adopt it — use the plainer alternative instead." The owner's reply settled it in one line: the
people who would have to use it did not know the tool the safe path depended on. The safe alternative had a zero adoption rate and the verdict reversed. Nothing in the vet
could have surfaced that; only the person who knows the users can.
